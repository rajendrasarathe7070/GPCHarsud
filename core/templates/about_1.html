Mr. Rajendra

From the Desk of the Principal
Dear Students, Faculty Members, Staff, Alumni, Industry Partners, and all Stakeholders of Government Polytechnic College,

It is a matter of great honour and deep responsibility for me to address you as the Principal of Government Polytechnic College. I assume this role with humility, gratitude, and a strong commitment to fostering excellence in technical and diploma education.

Our college is not merely an educational institution; it is a vibrant academic family, a center of skill development, and a stepping stone for thousands of students aspiring to build a successful technical career.

At this important stage, our responsibility is to move forward with clarity, confidence, and collective purpose. Technical education today must go beyond textbooks and traditional practicals; it must align with real-world industry requirements, promote hands-on skills, and empower students to solve practical engineering and technological challenges.

Our vision is to make this college a hub for skilled professionals and technicians who can drive regional growth. We are actively working toward strengthening the quality of teaching, upgrading our laboratories, enhancing campus discipline, and building strong ties with local industries to improve training and placement opportunities.

Students will always remain at the heart of all our efforts. Our goal is to nurture not only competent technicians and engineers but also responsible citizens and ethical professionals who can contribute meaningfully to the progress of our state and nation.

I seek the trust, cooperation, and active participation of all students, parents, faculty, and staff members in this journey. Together, with sincerity, discipline, and dedication, we can take Government Polytechnic College to new heights of success.
