About Our College

Premier Government Polytechnic of Nimar Region · Estd. 2008

Shaping the Future of Technical Education

Govt. Polytechnic College, Harsud is a premier technical institution under the Department of Technical Education, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The college was established in 2008 with the objective of providing quality diploma-level technical education to the youth of the Nimar region — especially the rural and tribal population of Khandwa and Burhanpur districts.

Affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV), Bhopal and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, the college offers four AICTE-approved diploma programmes.

With experienced faculty, well-equipped laboratories, and a disciplined green campus, the college continues to uphold the Government’s vision of “Skill India”.
